# Diageo Bar Academy — Advanced Cocktail Mastery: Craft Reference

Source: Advanced Cocktail Mastery workbook (238 slides). Philosophy: "outcome > process".

## §1 Core methods
- **Build** — in the glass, over ice (Highball, Rickey). "Ice last" for large rounds.
- **Stir** — spirit/liqueur drinks (no fresh juice). Velvety texture. Slower (up to 2 min); a frozen glass slows dilution; serve cold, immediately.
- **Shake** — anything with fresh juice / egg white / aeration. **Hard 15-20s** → −5…−10°C, then plateau (longer = pointless, except for texture).
- **Throw** — pour in an arc between two vessels; aeration + aroma without over-dilution. Good for Vermouth/Sherry, Manhattan/Martini.
- **Measuring** — Diageo recommends **jigger/precision** over free-pour. Practice with a scale (1 ml water = 1 g; 25 ml = 25 g; 1 oz = 29.6 g).

**Dilution:** large cubes = slow chill, minimal dilution · small cubes = fast · crushed = max (watery risk) · frozen glass = slows it. Typical: stirred ≈18%, shaken citrus ≈25%.

## §2 Advanced techniques (mechanism + ratio)
- **Fat wash** — melted fat ~25-50 g / 750 ml spirit, rest, **freeze**, remove the fat, coffee-filter.
- **Rice wash** — ~100 g rice / 750 ml; creamy, subtle sweetness. Consume within days.
- **Infusion** — botanicals in a solvent, sealed jar, dark, shake daily. *Cold* (delicate), *Hot* (tough, don't boil), *Rapid* (siphon + N₂O, minutes).
- **Sous-vide infusion** — vac + bath 55-60°C, 1-3h (spirits).
- **Clarification** — *gelatine* (~1 tsp/240 ml, set → freeze-thaw through a strainer) · *agar* (vegan, ~85°C) · *centrifuge* (4-5k RPM, 15-20 min) · *milk punch* (citrus curd, filter the curds).
- **Smoke** — gun under a cloche · smoked glass · smoked ice (~30 min then freeze) · tableside dome · spirit infusion (10-15 min).
- **Rotovap** — low-temp distillation under vacuum (bath ~30-40°C) → concentrated aromas, non-alc distillates.
- **Spherification** — *basic*: alginate 0.5-1% in the liquid → CaCl₂ bath 1-2% · *reverse*: calcium in the liquid → alginate bath 0.5-1% (acidic/alcoholic liquids).
- **Foaming** — *airs* (lecithin 1-2%) · *siphon* (cream/egg white/aquafaba + N₂, ⅔ full, chilled ≥30 min).
- **Dehydrating** — 50-70°C; dehydrator 50-60°C, 8-24h (garnish, powders, fruit leathers).

## §3 Homemades (exact ratios)
- **Simple syrup** 1:1 sugar:water · **sous-vide** 1:1 at 55-60°C, 1-2h · **fruit/purée** cook/blend + strain.
- **House bitters** — botanicals in high-proof alcohol, weeks, + sweetener + water.
- **Saline 10%** — 10 g salt / 100 ml; dose 2-3 drops (suppresses bitterness, lifts sweetness, umami).
- **Acid solution 10%** — 10 g citric / 90 ml; drop by drop.
- **Super Juice** — citrus zest + water + **citric 10-12 g/L + malic 2-3 g/L**, blend, strain, + 30-60 ml fresh juice/L. Fridge 2 weeks.
- **Acids**: citric (lemon/lime) · malic (apple) · tartaric (grape/wine-like) · lactic (creamy) · acetic (shrubs).
- **Foaming agents**: egg white (dry-shake 10-15s → wet 15-20s) · aquafaba (~30 ml, vegan) · lecithin (1-2%).

## §4 "Core Eight" — cocktail families (the creation engine)
1. **Highball** — spirit + carbonated mixer 1:2/1:3, tall, cubed. (Fresh juice → not a highball anymore.)
2. **Collins/Fizz/Rickey** — spirit + citrus + (sugar) + soda. Collins=lemon, tall · Rickey=lime, no sugar, built.
3. **Julep** — spirit (Bourbon) + mint + sugar, crushed, churned, metal cup.
4. **Sling** — modern: shaken with modifiers/juice/soda.
5. **Sour/Daisy** — spirit + sweet + sour (± egg white). Daisy + liqueur/syrup + soda (Margarita, Sidecar). **The foundational template.**
6. **Martini** — gin/vodka + dry vermouth, stir & strain, frozen V-glass.
7. **Flip** — spirit + whole egg + sugar, shaken, nutmeg.
8. **Punch** — spirit + citrus + sugar + water + spice; batched (Cups: Pimm's).
*Exceptions:* Old Fashioned, Treacle, Irish Coffee, Hot Toddy, Sangria.
**Balance:** "spirit, sweet & sour" + salt/acid as levers.

## §5 Gating by bartender level
- **Beginner** → classic Core Eight; build/stir/shake; simple homemades (syrup, citrus). No equipment techniques.
- **Intermediate** → + infusions, super juice, acid/saline solutions, throw, simple batching.
- **Advanced** → + fat wash, clarification, sous-vide, rotovap, smoke, spherification, airs — **ONLY if they're in `profile.techniques`/`equipment`**.
> Golden rule: never propose a technique/equipment the venue's profile doesn't have.
